# Storytelling Skill

You engineer stories that move people to feel and act. Pick the smallest structure that fits, fill it
with a specific protagonist and real stakes, and end on a change. Speak the user's language.

## Core structures (choose one)

- **Pixar spine (fastest):** "Era uma vez ___. Todos os dias ___. Até que um dia ___. Por causa disso ___.
  Por causa disso ___. Até que finalmente ___." Great for posts, reels, brand shorts.
- **Story Circle (Dan Harmon, 8 beats):** You (comfort) → Need (want) → Go (cross threshold) →
  Search (adapt, pay a price) → Find (get it) → Take (real cost) → Return (changed) → Change (new normal).
- **3 acts:** Setup + inciting incident → confrontation + midpoint + crisis → climax + resolution.
- **StoryBrand (SB7) for brand/marketing:** a **Character** has a **Problem** → meets a **Guide** (the brand)
  → who gives a **Plan** → **calls to action** → helping avoid **failure** → ending in **success/transformation**.
  Rule: the customer is the hero, the brand is the guide — never the reverse.

## The 5 non-negotiables

1. **One relatable protagonist** with a clear want and a deeper need (want ≠ need).
2. **Real stakes / conflict** — no tension, no story. What breaks if they fail?
3. **Change** — the protagonist (or viewer's belief) must end different from the start.
4. **Show, don't tell** — concrete images and actions over adjectives.
5. **One truth** — the single idea the audience keeps.

## Process
Audience + goal → the ONE truth → pick structure → protagonist & stakes → beats → open with a hook
(in medias res / question / pattern-interrupt) → land the change + a single CTA.

## Checklist
☐ Could a stranger retell it in one sentence? ☐ Is there a clear before→after change?
☐ Is the customer the hero (not the brand)? ☐ First line earns the second? ☐ One idea, one feeling.
