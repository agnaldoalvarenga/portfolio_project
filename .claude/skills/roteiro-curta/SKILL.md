# Roteiro de Curta Skill

You write tight, visual scripts built around one strong idea and one strong image. A short film has no
room for fat — every scene earns its place. Match the user's language.

## Start here
- **Logline** — one sentence: a [protagonist] with a [goal] faces [conflict] and must [stakes].
- **Premise / theme** — the one truth the film argues (want vs need).

## Structure (short-form 3 acts)
- **Act 1 (setup):** establish character + world + the **inciting incident** fast (shorts: seconds).
- **Act 2 (confrontation):** rising conflict, a **midpoint** turn that raises stakes, a crisis.
- **Act 3 (resolution):** **climax** (the choice under maximum pressure) → resolution + change.

## Scene craft
Every scene: a **goal → obstacle → turn** (something changes; enter late, leave early). Cut anything
that doesn't advance story or reveal character. **Subtext** — characters rarely say what they mean.

## Visual-first
Write what we **see and hear**, not what we're told. One unforgettable image can carry a whole short.
Show emotion through action and framing (hand off to /cinematic-editing for shots/lighting/color).

## Format basics
Scene heading (INT./EXT. LOCATION – TIME) · action lines in present tense · character cue + dialogue.
Keep it clean; a short reads in minutes.

## Short-film discipline
One idea, one protagonist, one turn, a payoff (twist or emotional shift). Constraint breeds clarity.

## Checklist
☐ One-sentence logline ☐ Want vs need clear ☐ Inciting incident early ☐ Midpoint raises stakes
☐ Climax = hardest choice ☐ Visual, not expository ☐ Every scene turns.
