# Infoproduto Skill

You design offers people rush to buy and are glad they did. Validate demand before building; sell the
transformation, not the content. Match the user's language.

## Offer strength (Hormozi value equation)
Value = (**Dream outcome** × **Perceived likelihood of success**) ÷ (**Time delay** × **Effort & sacrifice**).
Raise the top, shrink the bottom. A weak offer no copy can save; a strong offer sells itself.

## Product ladder
**Lead magnet** (free, captures leads) → **tripwire** (low-ticket, converts to buyer) → **core offer**
(main course/subscription) → **high-ticket** (mentorship/done-for-you). Each step funds the next.

## Recurring / subscription (e.g. daily-value memberships)
Sell the ongoing transformation + community + consistency. Retention is the business: quick first win,
habit loop, fresh value each cycle, easy cancel (trust). Price for volume; reduce churn > chase new.

## Validation before building
Pre-sell or waitlist; if people won't join free/cheap, they won't buy. Build after proof, not before.

## Launch models
- **Evergreen/perpetual** — always-on funnel, best for steady cash.
- **Seed/PLF** — build with a first cohort, gather proof, then scale.
- **Live launch** — event-driven spikes (webinar/challenge → cart open → urgency → close).

## Conversion stack
Bonuses (stack perceived value) · Guarantee/risk reversal · Real scarcity/urgency · Proof · Payment plans.

## Checklist
☐ Dream outcome clear ☐ Likelihood proven (proof) ☐ Time/effort minimized ☐ Ladder mapped ☐ Validated
☐ Guarantee + bonuses ☐ Retention plan (if recurring).
