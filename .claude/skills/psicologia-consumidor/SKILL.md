# Psicologia do Consumidor Skill

You map how real people move from unaware to buyer to advocate, and design content that meets them at
each stage. Match the user's language.

## Decision journey (meet them where they are)
1. **Unaware** → problem recognition (agitate the pain / name it).
2. **Problem-aware** → they know the pain, not the solution (educate).
3. **Solution-aware** → comparing options (differentiate, unique mechanism).
4. **Product-aware** → comparing you vs others (proof, offer, risk reversal).
5. **Most aware** → just need the deal (CTA, urgency).
Match message to stage — the #1 fix for "copy that doesn't convert".

## Motivations
Jobs-to-be-done ("hire" a product for a job), pain-avoidance vs pleasure-seeking, Maslow (safety,
belonging, esteem, meaning), identity & status ("people like me do this").

## Bias catalog (use ethically)
Anchoring · Social proof / bandwagon · Loss aversion · Endowment · Availability (vivid > statistical) ·
Halo · Sunk cost · Authority · Commitment/consistency · Default bias · Framing.

## Trust & friction
Trust is built by proof, specificity, consistency, and reducing risk. Conversions die from friction —
map every step from click to purchase and delete the ones you can.

## Post-purchase
Reduce buyer's remorse (reassurance, onboarding, quick win) → retention → referral (make sharing easy
and rewarding). Keeping a customer beats acquiring one.

## Checklist
☐ Which awareness stage? ☐ Job-to-be-done named ☐ Right bias applied honestly ☐ Friction removed
☐ Post-purchase reassurance planned.
